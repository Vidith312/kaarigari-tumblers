Our craft

Made slowly,
so it lasts.

Every tumbler starts as one sheet of 18/8 stainless steel. We press it into a double wall, seal it, then sand and polish each piece by hand. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is stuck on. The mark is fired into the surface, so it stays through every wash and every year.

  1. 01

    Shaped

    One piece of steel, pressed into a double wall that keeps chai hot till the afternoon.

  2. 02

    Finished

    Sanded, buffed and checked by hand. Small variations are part of the charm.

  3. 03

    Kept

    A five-year promise, a repairable lid, and a finish that only gets better with use.

The ritual

Warm from first light to last sip.

A tumbler is a small thing. But the right one becomes part of the day.

7:00 in the morning

First pour

1:00 in the afternoon

Still warm

Six hours in, it's exactly as you left it. No reheating, no microwaving, no rush.

9:00 in the evening

Last sip

Herbal tea or decaf. A small, warm full stop at the end of a long day.
